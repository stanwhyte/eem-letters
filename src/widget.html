Your letter

Offices count identical letters as one. The two or three sentences you add yourself are what makes staff read it.

A sentence or two is plenty. What you watch, what your family relies on, what you would lose.

Sign your letter

Parliamentary offices only act on letters from a named constituent at a real address in the riding.

Sending it yourself carries a little more weight, because it arrives from your own address. Either way the letter is the same.
